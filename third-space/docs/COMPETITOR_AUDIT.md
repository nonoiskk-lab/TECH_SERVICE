# THIRD SPACE: Competitor Audit + Super Website Blueprint

Markdown export of the audit dated 19 Nov 2026 (author: Sandeep Kumar Jaiswal). The site in `third-space/` uses this audit for **patterns only**: no competitor text, images, layouts or brand names are copied into the site.

## 1. Verdict

No single competitor is the "super website"; the winning model is a hybrid.

**What to borrow from whom**

- **Booking mechanics:** Yanisa Studios. 15-minute slot hold, itemised checkout, Razorpay, tiered cancellation.
- **Occasion-led theatre selling:** The Binge Town. Pick a theme, add add-ons, take a slot; decor from ₹600; ₹750 advance to reserve.
- **Price transparency and estimator:** PrimeTalk Studios. Sticky "From" price, live cost estimator, free 10-minute tour.
- **Format picker and prepaid hour packs:** DrBomb.
- **Refund tiers in plain words:** CherishX. 3+ days before: 90%; 24 hours to 3 days: 50%; after that: 0%.

**Three market gaps**

1. **Film licensing:** none of the theatre sites opened talks about a licence. THIRD SPACE's Legal Gate can be a differentiator.
2. **Theatre + podcast in one booking engine:** no Indian operator found. Closest is JBG Studio (Los Angeles).
3. **Dhanbad:** only multiplexes found (INOX, Desbandhu), no private-theatre operator. Confirm on Google Maps.

**Booking software:** no competitor runs a ready-made "theatre + studio" tool. Ready-made studio tools (StudioDock, LemonBooking) list Stripe only, not Razorpay/UPI, so a custom build is right. Plan B is in section 4.

## 3. Deep audit

Biggest pattern: the businesses that sell most show **price, slot and payment on one screen**. Trust drops quickly where content is incomplete or contradictory.

| # | Business | Good | Weak | Lesson for THIRD SPACE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Binge Town | Guest streams from own OTT account, company sets up; ₹750 advance; decor from ₹600; 13,000+ reviews, 4.9 (self-reported); 58 theatres in 6 cities | Homepage body text not readable (metadata only); figures promotional | Small advance + decor add-ons at booking time drive conversion |
| 2 | BingeWatch | Refund, Privacy, Terms, FAQ in navigation; WhatsApp and call; SEO blogs | No price or package list on the page opened; no booking steps | Local SEO blogs and policy pages from day 1 |
| 3 | ShowCity | Step-by-step booking guide; add-on list; 2 to 15 people | Price page reads like an unfinished brief; two booking domains; no refund text, reviews or content policy | Never publish placeholder text; one booking domain |
| 4 | CherishX | All-inclusive price; refund tiers 90/50/0; ID mandatory; "pirated content not allowed"; 4.6/5 (5,000+ Google reviews); WhatsApp | Content from guest's own streaming account, licence question remains; payment provider not named | Write rules clearly: ID, outside food, power-cut time extension |
| 5 | Roast CCx | Cafe + theatre together; up to 37 guests; corporate/watch party | No price or slots on listing; "Event ended" while date range continues; no licensing mention | Food + theatre bundle works, but show price and slots |
| 6 | DrBomb | Format picker; prepaid packs (6 or 10 h, valid 6 months); "GST included"; booking ID on email + WhatsApp; instant vs quote split | Planner does not reserve a slot; different prices in different sources | Creator Plan = hour pack; one source for price |
| 7 | PrimeTalk | Sticky price bar; estimator and package quiz; free 10-min tour; session timeline; UPI/cards/Razorpay | Contradicting prices and hours; unverifiable claims; hand-off to external booking tool | Hours and prices from one config; no superlatives |
| 8 | Yanisa Studios | 15-min hold; itemised checkout (price, add-ons, GST, 2% fee); tiered cancellation; 4-step how-it-works; FAQ | No live calendar or inline chat on listing; 2% fee never refunded | Copy hold and itemisation; add a calendar view |
| 9 | Dhivas | Lowest friction: duration + slot, name and email, instant confirmation | No phone or consent asked | Friction baseline; add phone and consent |
| 10 | JBG Studio | Screening room + podcast gear + crew in one venue; flat add-ons; 24 h bookable; 30-day cancellation; 5.0 on Peerspace (14 reviews) | Marketplace owns brand and data; instant vs request unclear | Hybrid thesis validated; keep your own site and data |

No podcast site opened mentioned recording consent; file delivery only appeared in Yanisa's "get your files" step. Treat this gap as likely, not certain.

## 6. Super Website blueprint

Target: a mobile-first site where a guest sees the price, picks a slot and pays an advance in 60 seconds.

| Page | Contents | Pattern source |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Hero ("THINK • SPEAK • INSPIRE", launch 25 Nov), Watch / Create / Celebrate picker, sticky "From ₹799" bar, 4-step how-it-works, FAQ near booking, WhatsApp button | DrBomb, PrimeTalk, Yanisa, MWS India |
| Watch | Package cards, themes, add-on prices, capacity, room photos, content rule per Legal Gate | Binge Town, CherishX |
| Create | Packages, gear, estimator, Creator Plan, raw-file delivery and consent explained, "Stories of Dhanbad" | DrBomb, PrimeTalk |
| Celebrate | Birthday/anniversary builder; custom decor via quote | Binge Town; DrBomb |
| Book | Package, date, slot, add-ons, details, itemised total, 15-min hold, Razorpay advance, consent, confirmation | Yanisa, Binge Town, CherishX |
| Visit the room | Free 10-min tour as zero-price booking | PrimeTalk |
| Rules and Policies | ID, outside food, power cut extension, cancellation tiers, privacy, recording consent | CherishX, Yanisa |
| Contact | Purpose dropdown, WhatsApp, map | Habitat |
| Blog | "Private theatre in Dhanbad", "podcast studio in Dhanbad" | BingeWatch |

**8 conversion rules**

1. Price on the homepage; "contact for quote" only for custom decor and corporate.
2. Estimator and checkout are the same thing.
3. Itemised total before paying: base, add-ons, tax, advance, balance at venue.
4. Refund tiers and ID/food rules before checkout, in plain Hinglish.
5. Booking ID on WhatsApp and email for every confirmation.
6. Hours, prices and capacity from one config.
7. Only real reviews and ratings (Google); no "100%" claims.
8. If the Legal Gate is closed on launch day: "Podcast Studio bookable now", Theatre "Coming soon", no screening promotion.

**Anti-patterns to check in CI:** placeholder text, price/hours contradictions, visible errors or dead links, two booking domains, unverifiable superlatives.
