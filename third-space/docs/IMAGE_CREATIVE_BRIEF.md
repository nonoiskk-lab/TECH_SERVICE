# THIRD SPACE: Image creative brief (what kind of images, in full detail)

Companion to `IMAGE_ASSET_PLAN.md` (where) and `IMAGE_SIZE_SHEET.md` (sizes). This file says **what each picture should look like**. Hand it to the photographer, designer or editor.

## 1. The look in one paragraph

THIRD SPACE is "the third place" between home and work: private, warm, safe and a little cinematic. Photos should feel **warm, clean, bright and personal**, not dark, neon or club-like. The site is orange and white, so pictures sit best when they have warm light (orange, amber, cream), clean white or cream walls, and one small dash of orange somewhere (a cushion, a light, a prop). Family-friendly: every picture must be fine for a couple, a family with children, a group of friends or a solo creator.

**Colour palette inside photos:** cream and warm white (60%), warm wood or dark ink neutrals (25%), orange and amber accents (10%), small teal or ember accents for studio and theatre (5%). Avoid heavy blue, purple or green colour casts.

**Light:** warm, 3000 K practical lights (lamps, LED strips) in the theatre; soft daylight-like 4000 K in studios. No harsh flash, no visible bare tubes.

**Mood words:** cosy, private, premium but friendly, clean, welcoming, safe. **Not:** gloomy, party-club, luxury-hotel-cold, cluttered.

## 2. Style rules for all photos

1. Real venue, real props, real equipment. Nothing staged to look like a different, bigger place.
2. Shoot at eye level or slightly lower (seated eye height) for rooms; 35 mm equivalent for wide rooms, 50 mm for people and details. Keep verticals straight.
3. Clean first: no cables on the floor, no boxes, no personal items, no brand logos of other companies in frame (cover or turn them away).
4. People: natural, candid, smiling, looking at the screen, each other or the mic, not at the camera, unless it is a team portrait. Everyone in frame has signed a consent form.
5. Keep the left or right third calm (plain wall, soft blur) on hero and header photos so headings and buttons can sit there readable.
6. Colour grade: slightly warm, lifted shadows, no heavy filter, no HDR look.
7. No text, watermark, frame, border or logo burned into the picture. We add text in code.
8. One vertical (4:5 or 9:16) version of every hero-type photo, for phones and reels.
9. For the theatre screen: show a neutral glow, the room's own title card or a blank screen. **Never a real film frame or poster** (licence).

### 3.2 Service pages

**Private Theatre (and "in Dhanbad" page)**
1. Header: wide room, screen on, lights low-warm, empty seats (1920x1080).
2. Screen and projector close-up (no film frame): "HD screen and surround sound".
3. Seating close-up: cushions, blanket, armrest with cup holder.
4. Lighting modes: same room in three moods (warm white, soft orange, romantic dim), shown as three small frames.
5. Snack and add-on table: popcorn, soft drinks, cake option.
6. Entrance and private door with the room name board.
7. Couple, family and friends variants: three candid shots.
8. Floor plan or seating diagram (illustration, vector).

**Podcast Studio (and "in Dhanbad" page)**
1. Header: wide studio, desk with 2 to 4 mics, acoustic panels, on-air sign lit.
2. Mic close-up with pop filter and boom arm.
3. Headphones and audio interface close-up.
4. Camera and lights set for video podcast.
5. Two-person conversation, mid shot.
6. Set variants: sofa set, desk set, standing set.
7. Control corner (mixer, monitor), tidy.

**Creative Studio**
1. Header: cyclorama or cream backdrop with soft lights.
2. Lighting kit close-up (softbox, ring light).
3. Green screen set.
4. Product shoot on a table, with a real finished sample photo shown on the monitor.
5. Reel shoot with phone on tripod.
6. Props shelf, tidy.

**Celebrations**
1. Header: best decorated room, wide.
2. Birthday set-up. 3. Anniversary: roses, candles (LED). 4. Proposal: soft arch and light letters. 5. Kids party: balloons, colourful but tidy. 6. Surprise: the "reveal" moment with hands on a cake. 7. Cake table detail. 8. Name board close-up.

**Creator Plans**
One picture per tier showing more usage as the tier goes up: Starter (one creator, one mic), Pro (two people, more gear), Studio (full set with camera and lights). Same room, same angle, so the three look like a family.

## 5. Logo brief (for the designer)

- Keep the idea of "the doorway": a door opening inside a circle, meaning the third place between home and work. The current mark is a working placeholder.
- Must work in 4 colourways: orange circle with ink door (default), white circle for the deep-orange band, ink circle for one-colour print, ink circle on the bright-orange band.
- Readable at 16 px (favicon) and at 3 metres (signboard). One typeface (Poppins is already on the site), wordmark in capitals "THIRD SPACE" with wide letter-spacing, "SPACE" in deeper orange `#A84300` on white.
- Deliver SVG, PNG (transparent), and a one-page usage sheet (clear space, minimum size, do and do not).

## 6. Using generated (AI) images: allowed and not allowed

Allowed, if the owner agrees: abstract warm backgrounds, texture, illustration-style art, icons, and decorative scenes that **do not claim to be the venue**. Mark them in `docs/ASSET_LICENCES.md`.
Not allowed: a generated picture presented as the real room, real equipment, real staff or real guests; fake reviews with generated faces; anything copying another brand's look.

## 7. Delivery checklist for every picture

- Right size from `IMAGE_SIZE_SHEET.md`; original, uncropped copy kept.
- sRGB, no watermark, no text in the image.
- File name like `theatre-room-wide.jpg`; alt text written (English, plus a Hinglish line if used on the Hinglish site).
- Consent form on file for every person visible; licence note for every non-original item.
- Checked on a phone: subject stays visible when cropped to 4:5 and 1:1.

## 8. Suggested order of shooting and drawing

1. Day 1 (venue shoot): 3 room covers, home hero, theatre set, podcast set, creative set, exterior, reception.
2. Day 1 evening: candid people shots with consenting guests, celebration decor.
3. Designer: final logo set, 7 remaining illustrations, per-page share images.
4. After upload: we place them (service covers, hero, package thumbnails, gallery) and re-run every test.
