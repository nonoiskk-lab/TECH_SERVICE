## Rules for every file
1. Photos in sRGB, no watermark, no text burned in, warm natural colour.
2. Keep the main subject in the centre 70 percent (phones crop the sides).
3. Name files like `theatre-room-wide.jpg`, with a one-line alt text for each.
4. No faces without written consent; no film posters or film frames without a licence.
5. We compress to AVIF/WebP; the sizes above are the originals to supply.
