# THIRD SPACE: Image and Logo Asset Plan (design system v6.1, orange and white)

Date: 2026-11-27. Status: plan, plus a first batch of ORIGINAL vector assets already built (section 10). Real photos and the final logo are still the owner's.

## 0. Rules (read first)

1. **Real over generated.** Every photo of the venue, rooms, equipment, team and guests must be a real shoot. No stock photos pretending to be Third Space, no fake guests, no fake testimonials, no fake "as seen on" logos.
2. **No invented brand marks.** The logo files come from the owner (or from a designer the owner hires). The inline SVG mark in `src/components/site-header.tsx` (an orange circle with a doorway) is a **working placeholder** and stays until the owner approves a final logo. Do not copy any other company's logo, icon style or photography.
3. **Consent.** A person's face or voice is published only with written consent (a short form, kept by the owner). Minors only with a parent's written consent. This matters for the Gallery, Testimonials and Recordings.
4. **Alt text on everything.** Meaningful photo = a short sentence in English and Hinglish. Decorative image = `alt=""`. Logo in the header = the text "THIRD SPACE" next to it, so the mark itself is `aria-hidden`.
5. **Colour theme.** Orange and white (tokens in `src/styles/tokens.css`). Logo must exist in the versions listed in section 1 so it is readable on white, warm white, deep orange (`#A63500`) and bright orange (`#FF7A1A`). White on bright orange is only 2.6:1, so on orange the mark is ink (`#1C1410`) or the orange is the circle with an ink door, never white text on bright orange.
6. **Performance budget stays.** CLS 0.000 and LCP 1.24 s are the current numbers. Every image gets fixed `width`/`height` (or an aspect-ratio box), `next/image`, AVIF/WebP, `sizes`, and only the first hero image is `priority`. Budget: hero 160 KB, card 60 KB, thumbnail 25 KB.

## 5. Format, naming and storage

- Folder plan: `public/brand/` (logos), `public/img/{home,theatre,podcast,studio,celebrations,visit,about,packages}/`, `public/og/`. Admin-uploaded photos go to object storage (Supabase Storage or Vercel Blob; owner to choose), not to the repo.
- Names: `third-space-logo-primary.svg`, `third-space-logo-mark.svg`, `third-space-logo-white.svg`, `theatre-room-wide-1600.avif`.
- Formats: logos SVG; photos AVIF with WebP fallback via `next/image`; icons PNG; OG JPG.
- Remote images need `images.remotePatterns` in `next.config` for the storage host. Gallery `<img>` tags (in `GalleryGrid` and admin content) should move to `next/image` once files are real.
- Colour: photos graded warm, not heavily filtered. Keep text off busy areas, or use a solid plate.
- Licences: keep a `docs/ASSET_LICENCES.md` (file, source, licence, consent) for every asset. Fonts (Poppins, OFL) are already credited in the design doc.

## 9. Not allowed (to stay safe)

- Copying logos, icons, photos or layouts from other websites or from the design research list.
- Generated people or fake venue photos presented as real.
- Movie posters or film stills without a licence (theatre screen photos use a neutral or owned frame).
- Publishing any guest face, voice or recording without consent.
